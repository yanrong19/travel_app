# Fitline

A travel web app that shows, for each attraction, the factors people forget to weigh before adding it to a trip: weather and season, physical effort, vibe (touristy or local, crowds), logistics (real time, hours, booking) and real cost.

- **Audience:** travellers planning a city trip, on a laptop at home or a phone on the go.
- **Job:** decide quickly whether an attraction fits, and save the ones that do.
- **Scope now:** Tokyo only, 16 attractions, mock data. A data pipeline will replace `src/data/attractions.ts` later.
- **Not in scope yet:** personalised fit scores, day-by-day itinerary planning, booking.
- **Brand commitments:** photo-led, light and airy, rounded cards with frosted glass over photos (from the user's reference boards). Factor groups keep Tokyo Metro line colours.
