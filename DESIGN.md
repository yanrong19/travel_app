# Fitline design

Photo-led, light and airy, set by the user's reference boards (travel app mockups with big photos, rounded cards and frosted glass). Tokens live in `src/styles.css` `:root`.

## World
- **Ground** misty `#eef2f4`, white surfaces, ink `#10191b`, muted `#56636a`.
- **Primary** deep cedar green `#0e4f47` (buttons, saved state, map data areas). Hearts turn `#e0245e` when saved.
- **Factor groups** keep Tokyo Metro line colours as small marks only (station rings, meters, chart bars): Conditions `#009bbf`, Effort `#e60012`, Vibe `#9c5e31`, Logistics `#00a27a`, Cost `#f39700`. Each has `-soft`, `-track` and `-ink` tints.
- **Glass** only over photos: `rgba(255,255,255,.16–.24)`, 1px white 38% edge, `blur(18px) saturate(150%)`. On light ground use frost `rgba(255,255,255,.78)`.
- **Radius** 32 / 24 / 18 / 12, pills 999. **Shadows** soft and long, only on things that float over photos or the map.
- **Type** Anybody (variable width) for place names only, animated letter by letter by `KineticTitle`; Figtree for everything else; Zen Kaku Gothic New for Japanese.

## Signature moves
- **Photo morph**: the tapped card photo becomes the next page's hero (View Transitions, `MORPH_NAME`).
- **Metro line stepper** in the factor sheet: the five factor groups are stations; tapping one switches the sheet.

## Layout rules
- Home (`/`) is the world level of the map: one MapLibre view from globe to ward (`/map/<country>/tokyo/<ward>` for deeper levels, same component, map never rebuilt). The frosted title card holds "Know before you go", the pitch and the live status line, and morphs into the place name as you fly in. Destinations are tinted (Japan stronger) and Tokyo has a pulsing photo pin. Every 5 s the tour lights a destination facing the viewer and tethers a card (3-photo fan, kinetic name, one-liner) to it; touching the map pauses it, it resumes 5 s after release, and 5 s after coming back to the globe. Clicking a country or the card flies in; coming-soon countries get the card pinned with "Explore Tokyo meanwhile". One search follows the level: Tokyo, destinations, countries at world level. Destinations live in `src/data/destinations.ts`.
- Guides under the map: at Japan, Tokyo and ward level the page scrolls and a sheet slides up over the map, leaving a 90px peek of it. Its sticky bar ("Japan · 8 regions", "Taitō · 3 places") pins below the nav with a tiny outline map and a way back up; its peek holds the phone region chips at Japan and Tokyo's filter chips. Japan holds the region guide cards; Tokyo and a ward hold the ward guides (or the other wards), the top-picks rail (an EffectCards deck on phone) and every attraction with its search and filter chips, one filter state shared with the map's tickers. The old `/japan`, `/japan/tokyo` and `/japan/tokyo/<ward>` pages are gone: those URLs redirect to the matching map level with `#guides`, which opens on the guides. Countries without data still get a coming-soon page with their landmarks.
- Trips: the **Trips** tab (TopNav and the phone BottomTabs: Explore, Trips, Saved) replaced the old Tokyo tab; `/map/japan/tokyo` is still reached from the map. `/trips` lists saved trips as photo cards, `/trips/new` sets one up (places from the globe or search, then dates or a day count, who's going, pace, day start) and `/trips/:id` is the planner. "Add to trip" sits on the attraction page and the map's pin card.
- Planner: the map page's frame. A frosted trip bar (back, the name edited in place, place pills, settings) above the map search; the day panel on the right. Pins: the current day's stops in cedar, other days' in muted cedar `--c-plan-other` `#4d7d75`; the day dropdown's stop counts use the muted one. The panel opens with a day dropdown (a select-only combobox; each day reads "Day 3 · Tue 14 Apr" with its rain mark and stop count), then two sub-tabs in one pill, "Plan" and "Wet-weather plan" (cedar for the open one), which also decide where adds go. Plan holds the stops and the day summary; both end with the trip's Saved tray (add, "On Day M", remove with Undo). In the planner the pin card's heart becomes a "Save" / "Saved" toggle for the trip's Saved list. Replace mode is a cedar-soft banner; removals and replaces get an Undo toast.
- Planner map levels: pins show only at a place's attraction level (Tokyo; a country outside Japan; later, per country from the knowledge base, which will also say where a small country like Singapore shows its attractions). Above it, Japan and its regions show a count bubble per child, centred on it: cedar "Kantō · 16" where there are checked attractions, white "Kansai · coming soon" where not. Crowded bubbles drop the count, then step just above or below their place, then fold to a dot that opens on hover or focus; the ones with attractions are placed first. Under the search (under the place pills on phone) one row holds a frosted breadcrumb, "Japan › Kantō › Tokyo" with cedar links and the current place in ink, then the same children as white chips ("Kantō 16", "Kansai Soon"), wrapping on laptop and scrolling sideways on phone. The planner's pin card sits below that row.
- Day summary: one row per factor group in its line colour (Effort, Conditions, Vibe, Logistics, Cost) with small marks (effort bars, nature/city split, time-budget bar) and warning flags, under a one-line summary. Never one score.
- Rain: a likely-wet day (50% or more) gets a rain mark and "N% rain" in the day dropdown, and a rain mark on its Wet-weather plan sub-tab, in the Conditions blue, kept small. Rain-sensitive stops get a Conditions-soft pill worded from their rain rating: "Poor in rain · N% covered" when rated poor in rain, otherwise "Mostly open-air · N% covered". The wet-weather plan is its own sub-tab with the rain note, the list and its time and cost; while it's open, attractions that aren't rain-proof are greyed with "Not rain-proof". Wet-plan stops get no pin colour.
- Phone planner (≤ 760px): the trip bar, the search, a sideways-scrolling row of place pills and the map level row stack at the top; the day panel is a bottom sheet over the map, as on the map page. The page scrolls (`useGuideScroll`, with a taller peek); the sheet peeks with the day dropdown, the one-line summary and the whole sub-tab pill above the tab bar (the day list opens upward from there) and comes up under the map level row, where the dropdown pins with a frosted backing behind the bar; the sub-tabs open the sheet's content. Pin cards dock above the peek; the OpenStreetMap credit sits above the sheet, never under it.
- Attraction: sticky photo panel left, factor chips right; on phones the photo leads and a frosted save bar pins to the bottom.
- Map: MapLibre with OpenStreetMap data from OpenFreeMap (positron style, recoloured in `restyle()`). A shaded-relief globe at world level that spins until touched, a fly-to into Japan, then Tokyo tilted about 50 degrees with 3D buildings from zoom 12.5. Tickers (photo, name, must-pay price) float above; colliding ones fold to photo dots. The OpenStreetMap credit must stay visible.
- Photos need a dark neutral scrim under any white text. Credit every photo (`/credits` and on each hero).
